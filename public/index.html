Biography

Trance in his veins

As a teenager, Frank Watson witnessed the rise of the electronic scene — and fell completely in love with trance. He started DJing in 2007 and has been delivering energetic, uplifting trance and tech-trance ever since, across the Benelux scene and beyond.

As a producer he has collaborated with established names such as Ciro Visone, Fisical Project, Kriticall, Accelerator and Belgian trance legend DJ Jan, with releases on respected labels including Black Hole Recordings, Always Alive, Enhanced, Discover and Redux. His music has been picked up by shows and compilations such as Ori Uplift's Uplifting Only (world premiere of "Sun Vibration") and the legendary German D.Trance series.

His 2026 single "Sayonara" (DJ Jan vs. Frank Watson) landed on Chasing Dreams Music — part of Tiësto's Black Hole Recordings — and unreleased material is already hitting the big stage: an ID was played by Driftmoon at Luminosity 2026. Frank is also resident DJ at X-Ray FM, hosting trance every Friday and Saturday evening.
